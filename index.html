04 — Field notes

Keep the claims honest.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

NOTE / 01

Boot ≠ usable OS

A kernel reaching a prompt or framebuffer is not a functional iPhone-like system.

NOTE / 02

Drivers are the wall

Closed vendor firmware and missing platform drivers are major blockers.

NOTE / 03

Evidence first

Every compatibility claim should link to logs, source, or reproducible tests.
