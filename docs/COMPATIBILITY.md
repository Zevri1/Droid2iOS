## Result vocabulary

- **Not tested** — no experiment performed.
- **Blocked** — an identified prerequisite prevents the test.
- **Boot experiment** — a limited kernel or early-boot result; not a usable OS.
- **Partial** — a named subsystem works, with limitations documented.
- **Working** — the specific tested function is repeatable; scope must be stated.

Do not use a broad “supported” label without defining exactly what works.
