# Primary references

These references are starting points for research. They do not imply that the referenced software or specifications are compatible with Android devices.

## Apple and Darwin

- [Apple Open Source](https://opensource.apple.com/) — Apple's public open-source release portal.
- [Apple OSS Distributions: XNU](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu) — public source repository for XNU releases.
- [Apple OSS Distributions](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions) — repositories for other published Apple open-source components.

## ARM and firmware

- [Arm Developer](https://developer.arm.com/) — architecture and platform technical documentation.
- [UEFI Specifications](https://uefi.org/specifications) — firmware-interface specifications.
- [Device Tree](https://www.devicetree.org/) — device-tree standards and resources.

## Research discipline

Prefer primary specifications, source code, vendor documentation, and reproducible logs. Community posts can be useful leads, but should not be treated as definitive evidence without corroboration.

When citing a source in a research note, record the exact page or commit, access date, and the specific claim it supports.
